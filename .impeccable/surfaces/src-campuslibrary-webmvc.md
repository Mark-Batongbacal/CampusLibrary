## Surface

CampusLibrary Web MVC librarian admin, Operate mode. Librarians need to find and manage books and members and process open-loan returns. The first viewport prioritizes the active task, a readable record table or form, and clear action controls. Preserve the Books, Members, and Loans workflows from the project brief. No authentication, extra workflow, custom product branding, or schema change is in scope.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A familiar librarian workspace uses metro typographic tiles as a disciplined hierarchy for catalog, member, and loan tasks; the records remain conventional and scannable.
OWN-WORLD: Flat black shell with restrained cyan, magenta, and lime fields; bold cropped lowercase headings; dense, crisp typography. Color signals navigation, selection, and meaningful state, never replaces text.
STORY: Librarians navigate standard top-level sections, scan a semantic table, open a record form or detail page, then act with labeled buttons and clear validation/empty states. No fictional metrics or product capabilities.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Responsive standard navigation and one clear page title sit above the primary work area. On list pages a conventional filter-free table is the dominant region with a visible primary create action; small accent tiles may summarize the current section but cannot displace records or reduce table readability. Forms use standard labeled controls and inline validation.
